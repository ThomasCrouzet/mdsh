# Contributor Covenant 3.0 Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make our community welcoming, safe, and equitable for all.

We are committed to an environment that respects the dignity, rights, and contributions of every participant, regardless of race, ethnicity, caste, color, age, physical characteristics, neurodiversity, disability, sex or gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, language, philosophy or religion, national or social origin, socioeconomic position, level of education, or other status. The same privileges of participation extend to everyone who acts in good faith and follows this Covenant.

## Encouraged behavior

We agree to:

1. Respect the purpose of the project and its community spaces.
2. Engage kindly and honestly.
3. Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
4. Take responsibility for our actions and contributions.
5. Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
6. Repair harm when it occurs.
7. Promote the well-being of the community.

## Restricted behavior

Harassment, character attacks, stereotyping, discrimination, sexualization, disclosure of private information, threats, endangerment, impersonation, failure to credit sources, disruptive promotion, and other behavior that threatens the well-being of the community are not permitted.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a possible violation privately through [GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting](https://github.com/ThomasCrouzet/mdsh/security/advisories/new). Do not include sensitive incident details in a public issue. The solo maintainer receives these reports and will protect confidentiality while investigating available messages, logs, and witness accounts.

Depending on severity and history, enforcement may include a private warning, a temporary limitation on participation, a temporary suspension, or a permanent ban. Serious incidents may skip earlier steps. Decisions prioritize participant safety, proportionality, a clear record of the reasoning, and opportunities to repair harm where appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when a person officially represents the project in public or private spaces.
